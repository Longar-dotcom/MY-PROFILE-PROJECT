ABOUT ME!.....

My name is John Longar Abuoch. i am 26 years old, also I am a young South Sudanese who was born in 1999 during one of the most difficult periods in the history of South Sudan. My childhood was deeply affected by war, famine, displacement, and insecurity that spread across the entire country for many years. During this time, countless families lost their homes, loved ones, and livelihoods because of the ongoing conflict. Many innocent people, especially young children, women, and the elderly, suffered greatly from hunger, fear, violence, and lack of basic needs such as food, shelter, education, and healthcare. Growing up in such an environment was extremely difficult because every day was uncertain, and survival itself was a challenge for many families.

As a child, I witnessed the struggles that my family and many other South Sudanese families went through because of the conflict. Communities were constantly displaced, and people were forced to move from one place to another in search of safety and peace. The war and famine created a painful situation where many children could not access education or live a normal childhood. Despite these hardships, my family remained strong and continued to hope for a better future. The difficult experiences we faced taught me the value of resilience, perseverance, and unity even at a very young age. In 2010, when the situation in South Sudan became more dangerous and challenging, my siblings, stepmother, and I were forced to leave our home country and seek refuge in Kenya. We moved to Kakuma Refugee Camp, one of the largest refugee camps in the world, where thousands of refugees from different countries were living after escaping war and conflict. Leaving our homeland was not easy because we had to abandon the life we once knew and begin a completely new journey in a foreign environment. Although arriving in Kakuma brought us safety from the conflict, life in the refugee camp was still very hard.

Living in Kakuma Refugee Camp was also one of the most diffuilt things i have never imagine in life untill today because it came with many challenges. Resources were limited, and many families struggled to meet their daily needs. Access to food, clean water, healthcare, and proper shelter was often difficult. Life as a refugee required patience, courage, and determination because opportunities were limited, and many people were trying to survive under the same difficult conditions. However, despite these hardships, my family believed that education was the key to creating a better future.

I was determined to continue with my education, and with the little support of my family, I enrolled in school within the refugee camp. In 2011, I started my education at Shambe Primary School in Kakuma Refugee Camp. I began in Class One and continued my studies with dedication and determination. Even though learning conditions were not always easy due to overcrowded classrooms and limited educational resources, I remained focused on my studies because I understood the importance of education in transforming mylife and helping me achieve my dreams. Through hard work and commitment, I successfully completed my primary education in 2018 after finishing Class Eight.

After i completed my primary education, I continued with my studies at Greenlight refugees secondary school, where I enrolled in 2019. i was very determined to pursue my secondary education and work hard to achieve my goalsand never despite the challenges that come as being a refugee. i continued to study with dedication and perseverance, and in 2023, i successfully completed my secondary education, after finishing my high school diploma. This achievement was a significant milestonre in my life, and it gave me hope for a brighter future. despite the difficulties I faced as a refugee, I remained focused on my education and worked hard to overcome the obstacles in my path.

My journey from South Sudan to Kakuma Refugee Camp has shaped me into a resilient, hardworking, and determined young person. The challenges I experienced during my childhood taught me to appreciate every opportunity, remain hopeful during difficult times, and work hard toward achieving my goals. Today, my story continues to inspire me to pursue education, personal growth, and opportunities that can help me contribute positively to my community and support others facing similar struggles.
